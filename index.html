Services

County site plans

The site plan your county planning department asks for with a cultivation permit: parcel lines, setbacks, cultivation and canopy areas, structures, water storage and wells, roads, streams and their buffers, slopes, and the notes the ordinance requires. Drawn to the county’s checklist, with a structure list and vicinity map.

State premises and property diagrams

License diagrams for the state cannabis regulator: premises boundaries, canopy, immature plant, processing, harvest storage, administrative hold and pesticide storage areas, each labeled and dimensioned the way the reviewer reads them.

Canopy and lighting diagrams

Canopy square footage calculated and shown greenhouse by greenhouse, and lighting diagrams for mixed-light and indoor licenses.

Plot plans and building permit sheets

Plot plans and structure sheets for greenhouses, processing buildings, sheds, water tanks and the other farm buildings that go through a building permit.

Redlines and corrections

Reviewer comments answered and the plans revised. A lot of this work is a marked-up sheet that has to go back clean before a deadline, and we treat it that way.

Hemp farm and field maps

Field and lot maps for hemp growers, with the legal description and GPS coordinates the state or USDA hemp program asks for, wherever the hemp is grown.

Parcel and site mapping

Parcel lines, county GIS, aerial imagery and, where it helps, drone imagery pulled into one base map before a single structure is drawn, so the plan starts from where things actually are.

Consulting

The drawings are what most people call us for. The questions usually come next, and these are the ones we can answer from experience.

Cultivation

Outdoor, mixed-light, greenhouse and indoor layouts, water and storage planning, soil and hydroponic systems, and pest management that does not lean on chemicals.

Extraction

Choosing a method and equipment for the product you want to make, laying out the room, and planning it so the premises diagram and the building permit agree with it.

Product creation

Oils, tinctures, edibles and topicals: formulation, testing, labels and packaging that will hold up to the state’s rules.

Permit path

Which drawings you need, for which agency, in what order, so the county, the state and the building department are all looking at the same farm.

Questions we get

Do you grow or sell cannabis?

No. We draw plans and consult. We do not grow, process or sell cannabis or hemp, and we do not hold a cannabis license.

Where do you work?

Anywhere cannabis or hemp is legal. Hemp can be grown across the country under federal law, and cannabis is legal for medical or adult use in most of the country. Every set follows the county and state rules where the farm sits, wherever that is.

Do you need to come to the farm?

Usually not. Most plans are drawn from parcel data, county GIS, aerial imagery and your photos and measurements. If a project needs a site visit or drone imagery, we will say so up front.

Medical, adult-use or hemp?

All three. The drawings follow the agency's rules, and the type of license only changes which agency and which checklist.

What do you need from me to start?

The address or APN, the license type, what the agency asked for, and any existing plans, redlines or photos. A rough sketch of where everything sits on the property helps too.

Can you fix plans someone else drew?

Yes. Send the plans and the reviewer's comments. We revise them, or redraw them if that is faster.

Are the plans stamped by an engineer?

No. Most county site plans and state license diagrams do not need a seal. When an agency does require an engineer or surveyor, we work with yours.
